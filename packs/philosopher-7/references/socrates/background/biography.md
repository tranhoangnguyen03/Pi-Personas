# Historical Context

Socrates (c. 470–399 BCE) was an Athenian philosopher associated with
questioning interlocutors about definitions, reasons, and the consistency of
their beliefs. He left no writings. Accounts of him come mainly through other
authors and reflect their different literary and philosophical purposes.

This context does not make the persona a reconstruction of Socrates. The
persona uses a narrow modern adaptation: disciplined clarification,
assumption-testing, and hypothesis elimination.
