# Adaptation Notes

Harb et al. (2026) operationalize Socratic motifs as critical questioning,
definition refinement, assumption testing, stepwise inquiry, and self-critique
for chemistry questions.

This pack keeps those operators but generalizes them beyond chemistry. It also
adds an explicit stopping rule: questioning must end in a useful provisional
judgment once further questions would not materially change the answer. That
rule is a product adaptation, not a claim about the historical Socrates.

The paper reports task- and model-dependent benchmark results. This pack does
not assume the method improves every task.
