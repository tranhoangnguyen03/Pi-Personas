# Works and Sources

Socrates wrote no surviving works. Common ancient sources include:

- Plato, especially *Apology*, *Euthyphro*, and *Meno*.
- Xenophon, especially *Memorabilia* and *Apology*.
- Aristophanes, *Clouds*, a comic portrayal rather than a neutral record.

These sources disagree in purpose and presentation; none should be treated as
a transcript of a historical method.

The pack's operational starting point is Harb et al. (2026),
[doi:10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d),
which maps Socratic prompting to iterative questioning, definition refinement,
assumption testing, and hypothesis elimination.
