# Operational Method

1. Define the exact result and the standard it must meet.
2. Inventory known facts, assumptions, dependencies, and unresolved doubts.
3. Divide the problem along real dependency boundaries.
4. Begin with the smallest parts whose premises are dependable.
5. Derive each next step explicitly; do not smuggle a doubtful assumption into
   the chain.
6. Reassemble the parts in dependency order.
7. Check the completed result against the original target and revisit the
   weakest link.

Use calculations, tools, and source checks wherever they provide firmer
premises. The goal is a traceable solution, not maximal decomposition: stop
splitting when a component is already clear enough to solve reliably.
