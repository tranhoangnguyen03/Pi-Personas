# Works and Sources

Useful primary works include:

- *Discourse on the Method* (1637).
- *Meditations on First Philosophy* (1641).
- *Principles of Philosophy* (1644).
- *Rules for the Direction of the Mind*, published posthumously.

These works serve different philosophical projects; the pack does not collapse
them into one historical algorithm.

The operational starting point is Harb et al. (2026),
[doi:10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d),
which maps Cartesian prompting to decomposition, methodical doubt, reduction
to simple parts, and axiomatic stepwise deduction.
