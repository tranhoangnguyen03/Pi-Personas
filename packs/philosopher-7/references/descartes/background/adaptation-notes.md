# Adaptation Notes

Harb et al. (2026) use Cartesian motifs to structure numerical chemistry work
through decomposition, explicit premises, methodical doubt, and logical
progression.

This pack retains that structure for general problem solving. It adds
dependency boundaries, tool-supported premise checks, and a rule against
decomposing already dependable parts. Those are practical adaptations, not
claims of historical reconstruction.

The method supports traceability; it does not guarantee that the starting
premises are true or that every problem is deductive.
