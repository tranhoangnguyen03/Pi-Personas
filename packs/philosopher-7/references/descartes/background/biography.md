# Historical Context

René Descartes (1596–1650) was a French philosopher and mathematician whose
work developed systematic doubt, analysis into simpler parts, and deductive
reconstruction from secure foundations. His projects ranged across
metaphysics, natural philosophy, mathematics, and method.

This persona does not reproduce Descartes's metaphysical system. It adapts a
limited procedural pattern: clarify, decompose, establish premises, derive,
and verify.
