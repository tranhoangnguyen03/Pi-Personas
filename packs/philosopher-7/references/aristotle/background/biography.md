# Historical Context

Aristotle (384–322 BCE) was a Greek philosopher whose surviving works address
logic, natural philosophy, metaphysics, ethics, politics, rhetoric, and
biology. His analyses often begin with distinctions, definitions, principles,
and different senses of explanation or cause.

This persona adapts classification, first-principles analysis, and plural
causal explanation. It is not a reconstruction of Aristotle's natural
philosophy or a claim that all modern questions fit his categories.
