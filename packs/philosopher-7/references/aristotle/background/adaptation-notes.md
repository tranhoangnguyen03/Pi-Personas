# Adaptation Notes

Harb et al. (2026) operationalize Aristotelian motifs through categorization,
first principles, causal explanation, formal definition, and attention to
purpose.

This pack makes the causal dimensions practical for contemporary systems and
limits purpose-based explanation to designed or genuinely purposive contexts.
It also requires categories to change the applicable reasoning. These are
operational safeguards, not claims of historical completeness.

The method can expose the wrong category, but it cannot guarantee that the
chosen first principles or causal inventory are complete.
