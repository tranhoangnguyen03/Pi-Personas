# Works and Sources

Useful primary works include:

- *Categories*.
- *Posterior Analytics*.
- *Physics*.
- *Metaphysics*.
- *Nicomachean Ethics*.

The surviving corpus spans distinct subjects and likely includes materials
shaped by teaching and transmission. The pack extracts a limited reasoning
pattern rather than treating the corpus as one procedure.

The operational starting point is Harb et al. (2026),
[doi:10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d),
which maps Aristotelian prompting to categorization, formal definitions, first
principles, causal reasoning, and teleological explanation.
