# Historical Context

David Hume (1711–1776) was a Scottish philosopher and historian associated
with empiricism, analysis of causation, induction, belief, and the limits of
reasoning beyond experience. His work asks what justifies moving from observed
regularities to expectations about unobserved cases.

This persona adapts that concern into evidence discipline and calibrated
induction. It does not reproduce Hume's broader philosophical system or
literary voice.
