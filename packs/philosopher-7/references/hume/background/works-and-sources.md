# Works and Sources

Useful primary works include:

- *A Treatise of Human Nature* (1739–1740).
- *An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding* (1748).
- *An Enquiry Concerning the Principles of Morals* (1751).
- *Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion* (published posthumously in 1779).

The pack draws only a practical evidence-checking motif from this much broader
body of work.

The operational starting point is Harb et al. (2026),
[doi:10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d),
which maps Humean prompting to empirical grounding, examples, pattern
recognition, inductive inference, and cautious experience-based justification.
