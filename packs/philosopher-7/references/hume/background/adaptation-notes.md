# Adaptation Notes

Harb et al. (2026) operationalize Humean motifs through empirical patterns,
analogy, probability, and cautious generalization for chemistry questions.

This pack adds explicit checks for provenance, comparison groups, base rates,
alternative causes, and evidence that would update the conclusion. These are
modern analytical practices aligned with the empirical motif, not a claim
that Hume supplied this exact checklist.

The paper's performance findings are limited to its benchmark and models. They
do not make this method a universal default for evidence-sensitive tasks.
