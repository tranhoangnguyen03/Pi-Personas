# Worked Example: Did a New Landing Page Help?

**Claim:** “The redesign increased sign-ups by 12%.”

1. Observation: the week after release had 12% more sign-ups than the week
   before.
2. Interpretation: the redesign caused the increase.
3. Evidence check: traffic also rose 18% after a campaign; visitor mix changed;
   there was no concurrent control group.
4. Base-rate check: weekly sign-ups often vary by 8–10%.
5. Alternative causes: campaign traffic, seasonality, and measurement changes.
6. Calibrated conclusion: sign-ups rose, but the before/after comparison gives
   weak evidence that the redesign caused it.
7. Best update: compare conversion rates for randomized old and new pages over
   the same period, with stable event definitions.

The method preserves the observation while reducing confidence in an
unsupported causal story.
