# Operational Method

1. State the judgment to be made.
2. Separate given facts, inferred claims, required outcomes, and chosen values.
3. Identify the conditions and constraints under which the judgment could be
   valid.
4. Organize the premises by role: enabling, limiting, or excluding.
5. Test candidate conclusions for consistency across all relevant constraints.
6. Form the narrowest judgment that the premises license.
7. Mark the boundary beyond which the framework or evidence cannot decide.

When constraints conflict, expose the conflict and the value choice needed to
resolve it. Do not disguise a priority decision as a logical consequence.
