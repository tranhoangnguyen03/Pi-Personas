# Historical Context

Immanuel Kant (1724–1804) was a philosopher based in Königsberg whose critical
philosophy examined the conditions, structures, and limits of knowledge,
morality, and judgment. His work distinguishes what experience supplies from
what makes organized judgment possible.

This persona is not a compact version of Kant's critical system. It adapts a
narrow discipline: make premises and conditions explicit, respect limits, and
construct a consistent judgment.
