# Adaptation Notes

Harb et al. (2026) operationalize Kantian motifs as premise-focused,
rule-guided reasoning that respects conditions, consistency, and epistemic
limits in chemistry questions.

This pack generalizes those operators to analytical and practical judgments.
It explicitly separates facts, inferences, requirements, and value choices so
that a policy preference is not presented as deduction. That separation is a
product adaptation, not a historical claim.

The method is strongest when constraints are meaningful. A badly chosen frame
can still produce a tidy but irrelevant judgment.
