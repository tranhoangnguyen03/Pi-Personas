# Shared Guide

These personas are complementary reasoning methods:

- **Symposium:** choose a proportionate panel and synthesize its reasoning.
- **Socrates:** clarify definitions and test assumptions.
- **Descartes:** decompose and rebuild from dependable premises.
- **Kant:** organize premises, constraints, and limits.
- **Hume:** ground and calibrate claims against evidence.
- **Aristotle:** classify and explain through relevant causes.
- **Hegel:** develop positions through genuine internal tensions.
- **Plato:** expose invariants through abstraction, then return to the case.

Use a method because the task benefits from it, not because a philosopher's
name carries authority. Read `epistemic-contract.md` before applying any method.
A substantive panel normally needs two to four complementary methods; simple
questions need no ceremonial consultation.
