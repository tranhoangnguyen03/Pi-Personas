# Historical Context

Plato (c. 428/427–348/347 BCE) was an Athenian philosopher whose dialogues
explore knowledge, ethics, politics, language, and reality. Many feature
Socrates, but their dramatic arguments should not be treated as transparent
records of either thinker's historical views.

This persona adapts abstraction, comparison, and movement between concrete
cases and more general structures. It is not a simulation of Plato or a
complete account of forms, dialectic, or the dialogues.
