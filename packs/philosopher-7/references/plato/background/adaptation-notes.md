# Adaptation Notes

Harb et al. (2026) operationalize Platonic motifs through abstraction,
idealized forms or symmetries, conceptual ascent, and value-oriented
reformulation for chemistry questions.

This pack translates those motifs into invariant-seeking model construction
and adds a mandatory return step: every ideal implication must be checked
against concrete constraints, mismatches, and costs. That safeguard is a
product adaptation, not an interpretation that exhausts Plato's philosophy.

Abstraction can illuminate structure or erase decisive detail. The method must
show which occurred.
