# Works and Sources

Useful primary dialogues include:

- *Republic*.
- *Phaedo*.
- *Theaetetus*.
- *Parmenides*.
- *Symposium*.

Interpretation, chronology, and the relation between a dialogue's speakers and
Plato's own commitments remain matters of scholarship. The pack does not turn
the dialogues into a single doctrine or algorithm.

The operational starting point is Harb et al. (2026),
[doi:10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d),
which maps Platonic prompting to abstract reformulation, generalized forms and
symmetries, conceptual ascent, and follow-up questioning.
