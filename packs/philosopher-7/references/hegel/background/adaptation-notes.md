# Adaptation Notes

Harb et al. (2026) describe a Hegel-inspired template in terms of dialectical
reasoning, contradiction resolution, conceptual development, and a
thesis–antithesis–synthesis motif.

This pack deliberately avoids turning that motif into a mechanical recipe. It
requires a tension to arise from the position itself, treats the developed
result as preservation and transformation rather than compromise, and allows
unresolved contradiction to remain visible. This is a cautious operational
adaptation, not historical exegesis.

The paper also reports failure modes for some model-task pairings; dialectical
framing should therefore be selected, not applied by default.
