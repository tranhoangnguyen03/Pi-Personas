# Works and Sources

Useful primary works include:

- *Phenomenology of Spirit* (1807).
- *Science of Logic* (1812–1816).
- *Encyclopaedia of the Philosophical Sciences* (first edition 1817).
- *Elements of the Philosophy of Right* (1821).

These works develop dialectic in different contexts. The familiar
“thesis–antithesis–synthesis” slogan is not an adequate summary of Hegel's
method and should not be attributed to him as a standard recipe.

The operational starting point is Harb et al. (2026),
[doi:10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d](https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae792d),
which maps Hegelian prompting to contradiction, conceptual development, and
dialectical resolution.
