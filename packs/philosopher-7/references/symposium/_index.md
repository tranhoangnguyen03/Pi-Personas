# Symposium Method

Use Symposium to choose a proportionate philosophical panel and turn its
different analyses into one useful answer.

Available seed material:

- `method.md` — panel selection and coordination procedure.
- `synthesis-guide.md` — the required shape of a substantive synthesis.

Project-specific synthesis preferences belong in
`library/personal/symposium/`.

Do not make a simple answer ceremonial, default every question to one familiar
specialist, or erase disagreement for the appearance of unity.
